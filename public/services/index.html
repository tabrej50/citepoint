PRACTICE AREAS & CAPABILITIES

Engineering brand visibility in the answer economy.

We combine AI visibility research, structured content engineering, technical optimization, and authoritative citation building to help your brand become recommended across AI search engines.

01. AI Visibility Baseline Audit

Establish exactly how AI systems discover, describe, cite, and recommend your brand. We test your visibility across 50+ commercial prompts, compare your share of voice against 3 top competitors, and map your citation gaps across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.

02. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Structure your content, technical signals, and authority profile to maximize AI search recommendations. We deploy answer-first documentation, machine-readable JSON-LD entity graphs, and semantically dense copy engineered for neural retrieval.

03. Citation & Source Engineering

Get cited by the exact third-party publications, industry benchmarks, and databases that AI systems rely on during retrieval-augmented generation. We audit your presence across Reddit, G2, Capterra, Wikipedia/Wikidata, and specialized vertical publications.

04. Knowledge Graph Alignment

Clarify your brand entity so AI systems understand what your company does, who it serves, and how it compares. We resolve entity disambiguation, align Wikidata claims, and publish rich semantic triples.

05. Longitudinal AI Reputation Monitoring

Track your AI visibility, sentiment, recommendations, and competitive shifts over time. Receive automated alerts when competitors displace your citations or when model updates alter query responses.
