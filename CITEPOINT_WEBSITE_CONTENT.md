### 2.8 General FAQs (`FaqAccordion.jsx`)
1. **Q: What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?**  
   *A:* Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the discipline of optimizing a company’s digital footprint, brand entity relationships, and third-party authority so that artificial intelligence search platforms—such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, and Gemini—consistently cite, mention, and recommend the brand in response to relevant commercial user prompts.
2. **Q: How does GEO differ from traditional SEO?**  
   *A:* Traditional SEO focuses on optimizing web pages to rank in a list of ten blue links on search engine result pages (SERPs) based on keywords and backlink counts. GEO focuses on how Large Language Models (LLMs) synthesize information from diverse authoritative sources to formulate direct answers. GEO emphasizes semantic entity grounding, synthetic consensus, verified knowledge bases, and multi-source citation rather than raw keyword density.
3. **Q: How do AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity decide what to recommend?**  
   *A:* AI search engines utilize a combination of pre-trained knowledge weights and real-time Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). When a user asks a commercial question (e.g., 'What are the best enterprise identity governance platforms?'), the model retrieves top-ranked, trusted third-party consensus sources, extracts factual entity triples, and synthesizes a concise recommendation based on source authority and corroborated facts.
4. **Q: Can you guarantee our brand will rank #1 on ChatGPT or Perplexity?**  
   *A:* No ethical or credible agency can guarantee fixed rankings inside non-deterministic, third-party generative models. AI outputs depend on dynamic model weights, probabilistic token generation, continuous fine-tuning, and user context. Citepoint focuses on the controllable engineering variables that maximize citation probability: authoritative third-party coverage, structured entity schemas, technical crawl permissions, and hallucination remediation.
5. **Q: How long does it take to see measurable results from a GEO engagement?**  
   *A:* While live retrieval-based engines like Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can reflect newly indexed citations within 3 to 6 weeks, systemic improvements across pre-trained model weights (like standard ChatGPT releases) typically compound over 60 to 90 days as third-party consensus builds across authoritative industry nodes.
6. **Q: Why are traditional PR and SEO agencies struggling with AI search visibility?**  
   *A:* Traditional SEO agencies are built around keyword volume and backlink outreach, while PR agencies focus on press release distributions that LLMs often disregard as self-promotional promotional noise. GEO requires a specialized blend of knowledge graph engineering, semantic schema modeling, vector retrieval understanding, and technical crawler management that standard agencies do not possess.
7. **Q: What does the Citepoint AI Visibility Audit include?**  
   *A:* Our flagship audit provides an empirical assessment of your brand’s presence across 5 major AI platforms. It tests 100+ category buyer prompts, maps competitor recommendation shares, identifies critical hallucination risks, audits technical crawler accessibility, and delivers a prioritized 90-day remediation roadmap for your executive team.

* **Stage 5: Final Recommendation & Citation Attribution**  
  *The model synthesizes a concise, structured response, recommending the top 2–4 verified vendors and hyperlinking the most authoritative sources as footnoted citations.*

* **Section Tag:** `TERMINOLOGY & CONCEPTS`
* **Heading:** `The B2B GEO Glossary`
* **Description:** `Standardized definitions for the key technical and strategic concepts governing modern generative engine visibility.`

1. **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):**  
   *The discipline of optimizing digital content, entity relationships, and third-party authority signals to maximize a brand’s citation frequency and accuracy in AI-generated answers.*
2. **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):**  
   *A subset of search visibility focused on structuring answers so conversational voice or chat engines can retrieve and quote direct solutions without reformulation.*
3. **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):**  
   *An architecture where an LLM references external, real-time knowledge bases before formulating a response, ensuring up-to-date and domain-specific factual accuracy.*
4. **Entity Triple:**  
   *A fundamental unit of knowledge graph representation composed of a subject, predicate, and object (e.g., [Citepoint] [provides] [Generative Engine Optimization]).*
5. **Recommendation Share of Voice (SOV):**  
   *The percentage of simulated buyer prompts within a specific market category where an AI model explicitly recommends or cites a given brand.*
6. **Hallucination Defense:**  
   *The systematic process of eliminating contradictory or outdated web mentions to prevent generative models from inventing incorrect pricing, features, or company facts.*
7. **Synthetic Consensus:**  
   *The state where an AI model synthesizes agreement across multiple independent high-authority sources, leading it to treat a brand claim as an established industry fact.*
