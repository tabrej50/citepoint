METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE

A practical system for a changing search landscape.

We do not treat AI search as a black box. Citepoint applies disciplined information retrieval science and entity engineering to position your brand as the definitive answer for high-intent B2B buyers.

How Generative Engines Formulate Answers

1. Buyer asks a commercial query in natural language.
2. The AI retrieval system issues multiple search queries to search APIs and vector caches.
3. The model extracts text passages from top-ranking sources.
4. The synthesizer reads the corroborated consensus across passages.
5. The final generative response cites the authoritative sources and recommends the top providers.

The 4-Phase Operating System

Phase 01: Discover (Week 1)

We run comprehensive diagnostic benchmarks across OpenAI ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Perplexity Pro, and Google AI Overviews to map your brand's presence across 50+ buyer evaluation prompts.

Phase 02: Diagnose (Week 2)

We identify root causes of omission: hallucination catalogs, attribution gaps, fragmented schema markup, absence from authoritative corpora, and technical crawler barriers.

Phase 03: Build (Weeks 3–6)

We engineer semantic triples, answer-first knowledge documentation, machine-readable JSON-LD entity graphs, and strategic citations across high-authority third-party publications and review repositories.

Phase 04: Measure (Ongoing Retainer)

We conduct longitudinal tracking of share of voice, citation health, competitor displacement alerts, and downstream pipeline attribution across model update cycles.
