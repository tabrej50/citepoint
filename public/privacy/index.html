LEGAL & DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy & Security Standards

Last updated: October 2026. Effective date: January 1, 2026.

Citepoint ("Citepoint", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our clients, prospective clients, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, safeguard, and respect personal and commercial data across our platform and consulting engagements.

1. Strict Non-Training Guarantee

We believe enterprise brand intelligence must remain strictly confidential. Citepoint explicitly guarantees:

  • We never submit confidential client materials, proprietary product documentation, or internal messaging into public LLM training datasets.
  • All synthetic evaluation prompts sent to third-party model APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity) use zero-retention enterprise API endpoints where data is not used for model training.
  • Audit deliverables, competitive displacement data, and benchmark gap analyses remain the exclusive intellectual property of the client under mutual NDA.

2. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization and collect only the information necessary to evaluate enterprise generative visibility:

  • Contact Information: Name, business email, corporate domain, and phone number submitted via audit forms or briefing requests.
  • Corporate Public Assets: Canonical URLs, publicly available schema markup, press releases, and published product documentation submitted for visibility analysis.
  • Technical Logs: Standard web server request metadata (IP address, browser user-agent, referrers) strictly for site reliability and DDoS defense.

3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Execute generative engine visibility audits and benchmark reports.
  • Deliver executive briefings and ongoing monthly SOV tracking.
  • Respond to direct contact requests and customer support inquiries.
  • Comply with applicable legal, accounting, and regulatory requirements.

4. Data Rights: GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance

Regardless of your geographic location, we respect comprehensive data rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a complete export of any personal data we hold.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You may request immediate correction or permanent deletion of your data at any time.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell or share personal data with third-party data brokers under any circumstances.
